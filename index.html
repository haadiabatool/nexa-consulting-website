Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, concise answers to common questions about our business advisory services and engagement process.

What does a business consultant do?

A business consultant helps organizations identify operational bottlenecks, optimize financial performance, design scalable growth strategies, and implement structured solutions to complex corporate challenges.

Who can work with Nexa Advisory?

We partner with a diverse range of clients—from high-growth startups and established small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) to large corporate entities looking for specialized strategic advisory and restructuring.

What happens during the first consultation?

During our initial session, we review your current business challenges, discuss your immediate and long-term objectives, and explore how our tailored frameworks can align with your growth goals.

How much do consulting services cost?

Our pricing is customized based on the scope, complexity, and specific strategic deliverables required for your project. We offer flexible engagement models tailored to deliver maximum return on investment.

How do we get started?

Getting started is straightforward. Simply reach out through our contact form or book an introductory session, and our advisory team will connect with you within 24 hours to schedule your consultation.
